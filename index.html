    Good to know

    • Getting there

      Bayfront MRT (CE1 / DT16), Exit B — follow the underground link straight into Bayfront Plaza.

    • A classic loop

      Flower Dome → Cloud Forest → OCBC Skyway → Supertree Grove for Rhapsody → dinner at Satay by the Bay.

    • Beat the heat

      Outdoor gardens early or late; save the cooled conservatories for midday. Carry water and an umbrella.

    • Stay for the show

      Garden Rhapsody is free, nightly at 7:45 & 8:45 PM. Claim a spot under the Supertrees 15 minutes early.
